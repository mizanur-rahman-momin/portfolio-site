You do not need every channel. You need one that works.

Pick three channels. Test each with a small budget and a clear goal. Keep the
one that starts real conversations and drop the rest.

## Why most channel tests fail

Teams try everything at once. Then they cannot tell what worked.

- Ads run next to cold email.
- Social posts go out with no clear goal.
- Nothing is measured the same way.

After a month, there is activity but no answer.

## Start with three channels

Choose three that fit your product and your buyers.

- Cold email, if you can build a good list.
- LinkedIn outreach, if your buyers are active there.
- Communities and giveaways, if you want early users and feedback.
- Content and SEO, if you can wait for slower results.
- Paid ads, if you have budget to learn quickly.

Three is enough. More than three makes the results hard to read.

## Give each channel two weeks

Two weeks is long enough to see a signal and short enough to change course.

For each channel, decide before you start:

1. What you will send or publish.
2. How many people you will reach.
3. What counts as a real result.

A real result is a reply, a signup or a booking. Not an impression.

<Callout type="note" title="Same goal, different channels">
Use the same definition of success for every channel. Otherwise you will keep
the one that is easiest to measure, not the one that works.
</Callout>

## Keep a simple scorecard

A spreadsheet is enough.

| Channel | Effort | Replies | Signups | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cold email | Medium | | | |
| LinkedIn | Low | | | |
| Community | Low | | | |

Fill it in as you go. At the end of the month, the answer is usually obvious.

## Decide and commit

At the end of 30 days:

- Keep the channel with the best real results.
- Fix the one that was close, and test it again.
- Drop the rest without guilt.

Then put your time into the winner.

## What to avoid

- Changing the message every day.
- Judging a channel in three days.
- Measuring likes instead of replies.
- Running five channels and understanding none.
