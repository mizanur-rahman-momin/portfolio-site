## Overview

Sublix is a SaaS product I am building. It is a subscription and trial
reminder tool. People and teams add what they pay for, and Sublix reminds them
before a trial ends or a plan renews.

Sublix is **in development**. It is not shipped yet. This case study describes
the problem and the decisions so far, not a finished product.

## Problem

Most people lose track of what they pay for. Free trials turn into paid plans.
Small subscriptions renew quietly. The result is money spent on tools nobody
uses any more.

The same problem shows up in teams, where nobody is sure who owns which
subscription.

## Context

- The idea started as a simple reminder app and was renamed from SubAlert to
  Sublix.
- The goal is a real SaaS product, not a single-purpose reminder tool.
- I am building it with AI as a working partner, using a written specification.

## Goals

1. Show every subscription and trial in one place.
2. Send a reminder before a renewal or an expiry.
3. Make it useful for teams, not only individuals.

## Planned features

- **Upcoming renewals.** Subscriptions that are about to renew.
- **Expiring this week.** Trials and plans that end soon.
- **Spending.** Monthly and annual totals.
- Accounts, teams and an admin view.
- Notification channels: email, WhatsApp, Telegram and Slack.
- Custom categories and multiple currencies.
- Bulk actions and subscription scanning.

## Technical direction

The stack is Next.js, TypeScript and Supabase, with PostgreSQL for data and
Supabase Auth for accounts. I develop locally first and deploy when the build
is stable.

I write a clear specification before building. That keeps the product coherent
and lets AI coding tools work against a plan rather than a guess.

## Challenges

- **Keeping the scope honest.** It is easy to plan too much. The first version
  stays small and focused.
- **Reminders that people trust.** A reminder is only useful if it arrives on
  time, every time.
- **Team use.** Individuals and teams need different views of the same data.

## Lessons learned

- Writing the plan down first makes the build faster, not slower.
- Starting small is the only way to reach a first version that works.

## Technology

Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, PostgreSQL.
