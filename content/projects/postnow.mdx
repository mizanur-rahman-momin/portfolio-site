## Overview

PostNow is a SaaS product I am building for LinkedIn content. The idea is
simple: turn one good idea into several posts, without losing the writer's own
voice.

PostNow is **in development**. It is not shipped yet.

## Problem

Posting on LinkedIn is a habit, not a single task. Most people run out of ideas
or repeat themselves. Writing every post from scratch takes time they do not
have.

The tools that exist often produce generic text that sounds the same as
everyone else's.

## Context

- The people who need this most are founders and marketers who post to build
  trust, not to chase volume.
- The product should feel calm and simple, not like a content factory.
- I am building it myself, with AI as a working partner.

## Goals

1. Help a writer keep a steady posting habit.
2. Turn one idea into a small set of useful posts.
3. Keep the writer's own voice in the output.

## Approach

The product keeps a simple workflow: capture an idea, develop it, then turn it
into a few posts. It avoids the two failure modes of content tools — blank-page
pressure and generic output.

The design direction is modern, clean and professional. The brand avoids
literal platform logos and heavy effects.

## Technical direction

The stack is Next.js, TypeScript and Supabase. The first version focuses on the
core workflow. Everything else can come later.

## Challenges

- **Voice.** A tool that rewrites everything in its own style is not useful.
  The output has to sound like the writer.
- **Scope.** Content tools grow features quickly. The first version stays
  narrow on purpose.
- **Differentiation.** Many tools write posts. This one has to make the habit
  easier, not just the writing faster.

## Lessons learned

- A clear workflow matters more than a long feature list.
- Designing the product around one habit keeps the scope honest.

## Technology

Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase.
